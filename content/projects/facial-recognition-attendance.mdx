## The problem

Manual sign-in at chapter meetings was slow and error-prone, and the records still had to be cleaned up afterward.

## How it works

1. **Enrollment.** Each member's reference photo is encoded into a face embedding once.
2. **Detection.** OpenCV reads frames from the camera, and faces are located in each frame.
3. **Verification.** Each detected face is embedded and compared against the enrolled set. The closest match under a distance threshold is accepted.
4. **Logging.** Recognized members are written to an attendance record with Pandas, ready to export.

## Trade-offs

- **Privacy.** Face data is sensitive. Embeddings rather than raw photos, local processing, and opt-in enrollment matter as much as accuracy.
- **Threshold tuning.** A strict threshold rejects real members in bad lighting; a loose one accepts look-alikes. The right setting depends on the room and the camera.
